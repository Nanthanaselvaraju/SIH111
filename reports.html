Feed Advisories

The guidance cards below are general educational information, not individualized test results or validated safety limits.

Saved test reports

Sign in to view saved test reports.

Prototype model estimate

The Python model estimates a category from entered nutrition values. It was trained on generated demonstration data and is not validated on field samples.

Values to enter

Use moisture, crude protein, fibre, energy and pH values from a feed report or laboratory result. The form's accepted ranges are input bounds, not safety limits.

Contamination tests are not included

This software does not test for aflatoxin, mycotoxins, mould, adulteration or mineral deficiency. Use a qualified laboratory for these checks.

Silage demo

The optional MATLAB CSV shows simulated temperature, moisture and pH. Its configurable thresholds are illustrative only; no sensor is connected.

Practical review

Check the sample label, collection date and units before entering a value. Compare the estimate with the original report and seek expert advice when needed.

Storage basics

Keep feed protected from moisture, pests and direct contamination. Follow local agricultural guidance for storage and discard visibly spoiled feed safely.
